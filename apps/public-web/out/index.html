A conference for people who build on Postgres

PG Day Egypt is a community-run, one-day conference for everyone who builds on PostgreSQL — backend engineers, DBAs, data engineers, and curious developers across Egypt and the wider MENA region.

Expect a single track of talks from local and regional practitioners, real production war stories, hallway conversations that matter, and a genuinely warm, welcoming room for first-time speakers and first-time conference-goers alike.
